# V34.3 — witness classification
RFC3161 section 2.4.2 distinguishes accuracy from representation precision. Missing accuracy may be specified by TSA policy; ordering does not establish accuracy relative to UTC. Public FreeTSA CPS does not give a numeric bound in the reviewed text. Preserve V34.1 rejection and proof. No retroactive admission, no retry, no timer changes. Offline audit separates recorded cryptographic success, local receipt before entry and unresolved independent time accuracy. Future gate needs a documented accuracy bound or separately reviewed evidence policy. References: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161.html and https://www.freetsa.org/freetsa_cps.html. No private data included.
