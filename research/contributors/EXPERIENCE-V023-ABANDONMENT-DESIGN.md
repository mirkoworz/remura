# REMURA Experience v0.23 — proposal LAB — 20/11/2026 19:30 CET
## Expired pending event: design only, not implemented
The v0.20 three-store model cannot safely reuse an occupied pending revision or mark it committed merely to move past it. Preserve the candidate and any journal entry permanently. Use a new explicit generation/protocol in a separate fixture, never modify frozen v0.56 or migrate existing stores automatically.

A disposition-aware independent authority must atomically authenticate the revision, archive digest, journal head, prior authority tag and disposition (PUBLISHED or ABANDONED). For abandoned events bind expired candidate digest, prior intent ID, reason code, generation, explicit owner approval and authority timestamp. Reject legacy readers against the new generation; an ABANDONED head must not yield market data, even after clock regression. Never expose pending candidate or fall back to older revision. A fresh approved candidate may be appended at next unused sequence after verified abandonment. No deletion or rewriting of evidence. For interrupted archive-only candidate, validate exact retained candidate, append missing authenticated event evidence only after explicit abandonment authorization; then finalize authority disposition. For journal-ahead state verify existing journal, never alter its tag. Stale candidate cannot use ordinary publication recovery. Authority outside restore domain and protected against its own rollback required. If authority unavailable, fail closed. No automatic repair, time-based autoapproval, or production migration.

Test matrix (not run): archive-only and journal-ahead expiry; expiry boundary; wrong owner/digest/generation; missing intent; revocation at authority CAS; interrupted disposition before and after commit; duplicate acknowledgment; stale retry; rollback both primary files; rollback authority; old binaries; fresh next revision; no numeric market delivery from abandoned head; physical crash on separate authorized fixture. Need explicit schema/security review before implementation.
