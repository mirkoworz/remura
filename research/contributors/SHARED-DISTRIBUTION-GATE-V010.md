### Limits and cost model (fixture only)
Input <= 8192 UTF-8 bytes, output <= 16384 bytes, max 7 forex rows and 5 prices, snapshot age <= 15 min at start and delivery, explicit stale price flags recomputed at delivery. COT freshness classification as of snapshot time, not official publication. These are provisional example caps, not production freshness policy. Each authorized request makes one snapshot adapter read + two identity adapter calls; denied requests before read make zero snapshot reads. For two people making 10 explicit refreshes each daily: 20 service invocations, 20 snapshot reads and 40 identity checks, excluding login, assets, retries and other endpoints; no provider calls attributable to this path. Actual D1 queries, CPU, quota and billing are not determined by this model. Do not activate until current account allowances and bounded runtime behavior are checked.
